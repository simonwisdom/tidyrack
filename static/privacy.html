Tidyrack

Privacy

Tidyrack is a small app made by Simon Wisdom. This page says what it keeps about you, where, for how long, and how to delete it.

What Tidyrack keeps

  • Your photos, and the pictures made from them: a cut-out on a plain background and an AI-tidied catalogue picture, and any What if I… pictures you ask for (the garment redrawn by AI dyed or altered). An Outfit photo of you is kept whole as well, for On me, with any AI pictures of you wearing one of your items that you ask On me for.
  • Your items' details: type, colour, fabric, brand, size, what you paid, your notes, and any Vinted listing it wrote for you, whether you plan to keep, upcycle or sell it, and an upcycle's how-to.
  • Your OpenRouter key. When you sign in, OpenRouter gives Tidyrack a key that lets it use AI on your account. It's stored encrypted, with your OpenRouter user id, so your items are there next time. Tidyrack never sees your OpenRouter password or payment details.
  • One sign-in cookie (tidyrack_session), which keeps you signed in. It lasts 90 days from your last visit. While you're signing in, a second cookie holds the sign-in's check for up to 10 minutes. There are no tracking or advertising cookies, and no analytics.
  • When you added each photo in the last day, for the daily limit of photos.

Your browser also remembers your filters and sort order, on your device only.

Where it's kept

On Railway's servers in its EU West region (the Netherlands). Railway is an American company. The cut-outs are made there too.

To read an item's details, write a Vinted listing, tidy a picture, draw a What if I… picture or draw an item on you (On me, which also sends the Outfit photo you picked), Tidyrack sends the item's photos through OpenRouter to Google's Gemini models, on your own OpenRouter account. Both are American companies; what they keep is set by your OpenRouter account and its privacy settings, and by OpenRouter's privacy policy. When you add an item from a link, Tidyrack fetches that shop page itself.

Railway keeps short-lived server logs, as any host does: which page was asked for and when, not your photos.

How long

Until you delete it. Deleting a photo, an Outfit photo, an On me picture or an item deletes its pictures straight away. If Tidyrack closes, I'll delete everything.

Deleting it

Signed in, scroll to the bottom of Tidyrack and tap Delete my account. That deletes all your items, photos and pictures, your stored key and your sign-ins, at once, and signs you out. To keep a copy first, use Export, which gives you everything as one zip.

The key OpenRouter made for Tidyrack lives in your OpenRouter account, and an app isn't allowed to delete it. After deleting your account, Tidyrack links you to it; you can also delete it any time in OpenRouter's key settings (it's called "Tidyrack").

Feedback

If you send feedback, I keep your message, the page you sent it from, and your email if you give one, so I can read and answer it. Your IP address is stored only as a one-way hash, to stop spam. I delete feedback when the project closes.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold about you, or ask me to delete it, at simonwisdom@duck.com. If you're unhappy with the answer, you can complain to the ICO.
